## 📍 Detailed Daily Routes & Helpful Tips Tailored for Parents

### ☀️ DAY 1: Sunday (`11/29`) — Arrival · A warm-up evening stroll to see sights near the accommodation
* **Depart Florence at `13:28` ➔ arrive at `Roma Termini` around `15:00`**

* **`15:45 ~ 18:30` | Stroll among classic Roman fountains and piazzas at night (walk everywhere from the accommodation!)**
  1. **`Trevi Fountain`** *(8 minutes downhill on foot from the accommodation!)*: Enjoy it for free from the upper piazza or pay `€2` to toss a coin by the water.
  2. **`Spanish Steps`** *(9-minute walk)*: Enjoy the atmosphere of the steps and luxury shopping street lit with Christmas lights in late November *(※ beware: sitting on the steps may prompt police intervention and a fine!)*.

  * **`Dar Bruttone Rione Monti`** (`Via dei Serpenti, 113` · rating **4.8** · `19:00~23:00`): Right next to the accommodation! Top-quality versions of Rome's 3 great pastas (**carbonara · cacio e pepe · amatriciana**).

### ⛪ DAY 3: Tuesday (`12/1`) — Vatican City & Castel Sant'Angelo *(the perfect day of the week!)*
* **Why is Tuesday the right answer?**
  * On Wednesday (`12/2`) morning, **`St. Peter's Basilica`** is closed until `12:30` because of the **Papal Wednesday Audience**, and on Monday **`Castel Sant'Angelo`** is closed. **Tuesday (`12/1`)** is the only golden day to see the Vatican and Castel Sant'Angelo all at once!
* **🚌 Super-easy transport from in front of the accommodation (transport cost `€1.50` per person · card tap `Tap&Go` OK!):**
  * From the `Via Nazionale` stop right in front of the accommodation, take **bus `40` (express) or `64`** to go directly to just outside **`St. Peter's Basilica`** without a transfer! *(Or take Metro Line A from `Repubblica` station and get off at `Ottaviano` station.)*
* **`08:15 ~ 13:30` | Focused visit to Vatican City (order to minimize parent fatigue!)**
  1. **`08:15` Enter `St. Peter's Basilica` first (`€0` free! · `07:00~19:10`):** In the 8 a.m. hour, the security line is shortest, at 10~15 minutes. First take your time to admire Michelangelo's 《Pietà》 and the overwhelming basilica interior. *(※ The 551 steps to the dome are steep, like those of Florence Cathedral, so parents are advised to skip it.)*
  2. **`10:30` or `11:00` `Vatican Museums` & Sistine Chapel (`08:00~20:00`):**
     * **Tickets:** Advance booking is essential on the official website (`tickets.museivaticani.va`), **`€25`** (`€20` + `€5` booking fee)! *(On-site `€20` tickets mean long lines · audio guide `€8` — Korean language supported!)*
     * **💡 Recommended tip:** The Vatican has an enormous number of works, so your parents' satisfaction will double if you take a **Korean-language half-day guided tour** (3~4 hours in the morning) or use the official Korean audio guide! *(※ Photography is completely prohibited inside the Sistine Chapel.)*
* **`13:30 ~ 14:45` | Lunch in Prati near the Vatican**
  * **Authentic pasta · meal:** **`Osteria Ragno d'oro`** (`Via Silla, 26` · rating **4.6** · Tue `12:30~15:00`) — a traditional Roman restaurant frequented by locals, a 5-minute walk from the Vatican!
  * **Value-for-money Roman pizza al taglio:** **`Bonci Pizzarium`** (`Via della Meloria, 43` · `11:15~22:00`) — Rome's No. 1 pizza al taglio restaurant near the Vatican Museums.
* **`15:00 ~ 17:15` | `Castel Sant'Angelo` & sunset (`16:39`) at the Bridge of Angels**
  * **`Castel Sant'Angelo`** (`09:00~19:30` · rating **4.7**): A 2nd-century mausoleum of Emperor Hadrian and a secret fortress of the Pope! The view from the top terrace is fantastic. *(If the admission fee feels burdensome, it is also wonderful to enjoy the Bridge of Angels outside, **`Ponte Sant'Angelo`**, and the Tiber at sunset for free!)*
  * On the way back, take **bus `40` or `64`** from the riverside directly to `Via Nazionale` (right in front of the accommodation!).

### ☕ DAY 4: Wednesday (`12/2`) — Baroque city center (Pantheon·Navona) + a day for the atmosphere of Trastevere
* **Weather:** Sunny and clear all day (`$0\%$`)! This is the most romantic day for strolling through Rome's lanes.
* **`09:30 ~ 12:30` | The key triangle of Rome's old town**
  1. **`Pantheon`** *(15-minute walk from the accommodation · `09:00~19:00` · adult **`€7`**)*: Admire the world's largest unreinforced concrete dome, 2,000 years old, sunlight streaming through the hole in the ceiling (oculus), and Raphael's tomb!
  2. **☕ Rome's 3 great coffees & gelato break (just 1~3 minutes on foot from the Pantheon!):**
     * **`La Casa del Caffè Tazza d'Oro`** (`Via degli Orfani, 84` · `07:00~20:00`): A destination for its signature coffee granita (coffee slush with whipped cream) and for buying `Regina dei Caffè` coffee beans as souvenirs!
     * **`Giolitti Gelato`** (`Via degli Uffici del Vicario, 40` · `07:30~24:00`): Rome's best traditional gelato *(rice flavor `Riso` · pistachio recommended)*.
  3. **`Piazza Navona`** *(4-minute walk)*: Rome's most elegant Baroque piazza, where Bernini's Fountain of the Four Rivers shines.
  4. **`Campo de' Fiori`** *(4-minute walk)*: Rome's signature open-air market, open from morning until `13:30` in the afternoon! *(Browse pasta · spices · fruit.)*
* **`13:00 ~ 15:30` | Lunch and a stroll through the lanes of `Trastevere`, across the Tiber**
  * **`Tonnarello`** (`V. della Paglia, 1/2/3` · rating **4.7**) or its branch **`Tonnarello Scala`** (`Piazza della Scala, 19/21` · rating **4.8` · both `11:30~23:00` with no break time!): Trastevere's undisputed top-tier Roman pasta and meatball restaurant!
  * **`Basilica of Santa Maria in Trastevere`** (`07:30~20:30` · **free**): One of Rome's oldest Marian basilicas, with dazzling 12th-century golden mosaics.
* **`16:00 ~ 17:15` | 🎬 [Bonus drama filming location!] Sunset view at `Fontana dell'Acqua Paola`**
  * The magnificent marble fountain **`Fontana dell'Acqua Paola`** on Janiculum Hill above Trastevere (free 24 hours) is a **key Rome filming location for 《Can This Love Be Translated?》** and Rome's best free sunset spot, overlooking all of central Rome! *(※ The uphill climb is steep, so take minibus `115` or a short taxi from Trastevere; if you're tired, simply returning to the accommodation is also perfectly fine!)*

Its golden ceiling, Bernini's tomb, and its significance as the burial place of Pope Francis make it meaningful.

  1. Ask the **`Salotto Monti`** front desk to pre-book the official taxi to the airport for tomorrow (`12/4 Fri`), with the **flat fare (`€55`)!**
